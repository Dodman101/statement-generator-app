Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019 requires data controllers and processors to register with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) - confirm your registration status and obligations with the ODPC or counsel before relying on this page, particularly if you or your clients operate outside Kenya too.

1. What we collect

  • Account information: a label for your account and a hashed copy of your API key. We never store your key in plain text - only a one-way hash, so we can verify it without being able to see it.
  • Files you upload: the Word template and spreadsheet you submit for a given job, and the PDF statements generated from them. These may contain personal data about the people named in your spreadsheet (names, ID numbers, and whatever else your template includes).
  • Job metadata: when a job ran, how many rows it processed, and whether it succeeded - used for enforcing plan limits and for our own troubleshooting. This does not include the content of your files.

2. How long we keep it

  • Uploaded templates and spreadsheets are deleted from disk as soon as processing finishes (success or failure) - they are never kept longer than the single job that used them.
  • Generated PDF statements are available for download for up to 2 hours after a job completes, after which they're automatically and permanently deleted.
  • Job metadata (row counts, timestamps, pass/fail status - not file content) is retained for usage tracking and billing history.

3. Where it's processed and stored

Files are processed on our application server (currently hosted on Render) and are not copied anywhere else. Account and job metadata is stored in a Postgres database (currently hosted on Neon). Each account's job records are isolated using database-level access controls (Row-Level Security), so one account's data cannot be read by a database query scoped to another account - even by mistake in our own code.

4. Who else sees it

We don't sell or share your data with third parties for marketing. The only parties with any access are infrastructure providers that host the Service (currently Render for compute and Neon for the database), who process data solely to provide that hosting.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you or your data subjects are located, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. Given our short retention window for files, most requests are already satisfied automatically - contact us if you need something specific (for example, deleting job metadata early).

6. Security

  • API keys are stored as one-way hashes, never in plain text.
  • All traffic to the Service is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
  • Optional password protection lets you lock each generated PDF with that row's own ID.
  • Account data is isolated at the database level using Row-Level Security, not just application-level checks.

7. Contact

Questions or requests about your data: legal@dodman.group.
